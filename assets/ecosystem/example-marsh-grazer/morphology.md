# Example marsh grazer morphology

## Ecological job

Consumes emergent marsh vegetation and moves nutrients between wet meadow and shallow water.

## Real-world referents

Capybara supplies the low amphibious body and blunt grazing head. Marsh deer supplies the leggy silhouette associated with flooded grassland.

## Silhouette bet

A player should read “wet-ground grazer” from its long clearance legs, low body, and unornamented grazing head.

## Trait map

| Trait | Environmental driver | Continuous or discrete | Visible expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| Limb length | Inundation and soft substrate | Continuous | Shared leg bones scale longer in persistently wetter habitat |
| Foot plan | Persistent inundation | Discrete | Compact and splayed-hoof authored variants |

## Family resemblance

Every variant retains the same blunt head, barrel torso, spine line, and marsh-umber palette.

## Rejected directions

- Antlers were rejected because they do not communicate the habitat adaptation this family exists to show.
